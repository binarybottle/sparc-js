📈 Expected Behavior:
  • /i/ (see): Small jaw opening, tongue forward/high
  • /a/ (cat): Large jaw opening, tongue low/back
  • /u/ (boot): Small jaw opening, lips protruded
  • /s/ (see): Tongue tip near teeth
🦴 Jaw Movement Examples:
  • 10% opening: /i/, /u/ (high vowels)
  • 30% opening: /e/, /o/ (mid vowels)
